How to Rank Up

🎯

Win Matches

Every 5v5 competitive win awards Elo. Losses deduct Elo. Consistent wins are the fastest path upward.

📈

Improve Your Rating

Performance matters. Higher K/D, damage, and objective play can earn bonus Elo on close games.

🛡️

Avoid Losing Streaks

Consecutive losses reduce Elo faster. Take breaks, reset, and requeue fresh.

🏆

Climb Each Sublevel

Each tier has 5 sublevels: V → IV → III → II → I. Reach I to promote to the next tier.
